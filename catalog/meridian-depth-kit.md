# Meridian Depth Kit

Working session on depth correction and tidal datum normalisation with the Meridian toolkit. The morning covers ingest and the afternoon is a clinic on your own survey data.

## Programme notes

Registration is through the studio office; tea and field rations are included. Sessions run rain or shine.
