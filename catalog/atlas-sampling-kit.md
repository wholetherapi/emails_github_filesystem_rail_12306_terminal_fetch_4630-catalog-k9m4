# Atlas Sampling Kit

A full-day field session on stratified site selection and reproducible sample handling, run with the Atlas toolkit. Bring a laptop; the afternoon block is spent on the riverfront plots.

## Programme notes

Registration is through the studio office; tea and field rations are included. Sessions run rain or shine.
